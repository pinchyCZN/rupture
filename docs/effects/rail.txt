PCDEMOS RUPTURE                                            Analysis Note


                         PART_RAIL: THE TRAIN


Abstract

   part_rail is slot 3 of the relay. A train runs along a track in open
   space: first close beside it, then through a passage of reaching
   hands, an engine of turning cylinders and a figure, then a long shot
   of the train on its rails. It runs from t = 0 to t = 65 on its own
   clock and hands over to part_boat.

1.  What it draws

   meshes/trainlong.obj as three display lists, one placed per car;
   boat.obj; the bridge and rails along one long track and five rail
   tracks, drawn by maybe_Track_DrawRailsAlt; hand.obj for
   24 < t < 28.2; cylinders.obj as the "engine" for 11 < t < 24; and
   human.obj for 40 <= t < 58 [verified, the meshes each section
   draws]. It looks like a night train in open space [observed, the
   original every 6 s of its clock, the shim's fixed-step mode].

2.  Init

   part_rail::Init (0x0049cb50) is part_truck::Init's shape in the slot
   band 3000: the same render targets under the same gate, the same
   four textures, box.3ds with the projection_anisotropic pair, five
   pairs (v_roadcut3 with f_bridge2, f_hand, f_head and f_engine;
   vertex_train with fragment_trainold2) and the six post passes
   [verified]. It loads hand, boat (into g_boatObj), trainlong,
   cylinders and human, and compiles cylinders' groups 0..5 into
   display lists 1000..1005 and trainlong's groups 0..2 into
   2000..2002, which DrawScene calls by those ids [verified]. One track
   at this+0x1460 has 2950 points from (0, -50, 850) stepping -0.7 in
   z; five rails at this+0x14c0 are part_truck's, the Euler turn's
   first angle -0.001 [verified]. No shards and no rand. Both clocks
   start at 0.

3.  Frame

   3.1.  The clock

   part_rail::Frame (0x0049d280) gates on started[3]/done[3]. tLocal
   (this+0x320) gains the frame delta, held at 0 at least, and t
   (this+0x318) is tLocal + g_partTimeCarry, with no hold-backs. nearZ
   is 0.45, farZ 550. At t = 65 the overshoot goes to g_partTimeCarry
   and part_boat starts; partEnd is t [verified; observed, the original
   ends the part at t = 64.99].

   3.2.  The cameras

   Two base cameras, then twelve windows, all tested on t: -10..6,
   6..11, 11..20, 20..24, 24..38, 38..40, 40..42, 42..44, 44..51,
   51..54, 54..58 and 58..158. Only 24..38 blends with Camera_Lerp,
   toward a second camera at clamp((t - 31) 0.28), as part_video's does
   [verified]. The expressions are in the source.

   3.3.  The shake

   As part_truck's: index trunc(35 t) mod n, plus 280 from t = 51 and
   plus 490 for 24 <= t < 38. RightV and UpV are doubled for
   28 < t < 42 and scaled by 2.2 from t = 51; bend3's alpha is 0.2
   [verified].

4.  DrawScene

   part_rail::DrawScene (0x00497100) is part_truck's in outline: the
   stars under srand(0xdc072526), each also following eye.z; the sun
   and glows anchored by the eye and moved at t = 51; the pass set-up;
   then the lit sections. The train cars from display lists 2000..2002
   and boat.obj, skipped for 24 < t < 31; the bridge and rails with
   maybe_Track_DrawRailsAlt, turned 188 or 202 degrees for their second
   draw; the hands; the engine from lists 1000..1005 in two passes; the
   figure; a glow trail as part_truck's with its own fade; and two sets
   of 1000 additive line streaks under srand(0x9600b3bf). The last
   glPopMatrix balances the push after the sky [verified].

   Several values cross DrawScene's sections through its stack frame:
   the train loop's per-car accumulators feed the bridge's "fireplace"
   uniform, and the glow block's Q, H and Z0 feed the streaks; the
   transcription keeps them as one set of locals [verified, the stack
   offsets at each access].

5.  Reconstruction notes

   src/rupture/demo/part_rail.cpp holds the constructor, Frame and
   Init; part_rail_draw.cpp DrawScene and maybe_Track_DrawRailsAlt.

   The port matches the original at every 6 s checkpoint at the
   800x450 view, to a mean of 0.25 per channel or less, except the
   engine, 19 < t < 24, at 3.7 [observed]. The engine's colour image
   matches; its normal image differs on some pistons, where lists
   1001 and 1000 draw near-coplanar faces at the same place and the
   inner one shows through [observed, the target 0 textures with the
   post passes skipped for a frame]. The 32-bit depth buffer
   (effects/look.txt 1) settles most of them; which face wins on the
   rest turns on the float rounding of the matrices, which the
   driver computes in the original and core/gl1 in the port
   [inferred].
