PCDEMOS RUPTURE                                            Analysis Note


                                 VIDEO


Abstract

   part_video: the opening room - shelves, a desk, an oval TV playing
   an animation and a wall of oval holes - which later breaks apart.
   Every surface is shaded with the edge-and-glow look
   (effects/look.txt), and two textures are laid over it by WORLD
   position rather than by the meshes' own coordinates: the TV's
   picture and the noise that dissolves the walls. Init 0x004b10c0,
   Frame 0x004b19c0, DrawScene 0x004ae0a0. src/rupture draws it, as
   slot 0 of its relay.

3.  DrawScene

   part_video::DrawScene(this, ctx, unused) draws, in order
   [verified]: maybe_Scene_DrawLit with the TV pair, the hex4 grid,
   maybe_Scene_DrawLit with the room pair, walls2, the walls.obj
   shards in immediate mode, three room2 copies and, before t = 11,
   a fourth, then the sky (3.5). t is the part's clock this+0x1b8; s
   is this+0x358, 0 in the opening [observed].

   maybe_Scene_DrawLit binds a pair, sets CameraPos (always the
   context's eye camera), LightPos (the light camera's eye),
   ModelViewProj and ModelViewLightProj (the light camera through
   gluPerspective(75 + ctx+0x608e38, 1, near, far), biased by
   translate(0.5) scale(0.5)), and hands box.3ds to maybe_Mesh_Draw,
   which binds decal, light and the material's texture2 and draws
   tris * ctx+0x1bd8 * 3 indices. DrawScene sets ctx+0x1bd8 to 0
   around both calls, so box.3ds is never drawn: the calls are only
   the pairs' set-up [verified, 0x00454fcc].

   3.4.  The shards

   The wall is broken in the modeller, not in code: walls.obj is a
   list of groups, and Obj_MakeShards (0x004670e0) turns every group
   but the last into one shard [verified]. A shard is a TETRAHEDRON
   on four corners - the first face's c[0], c[1], c[2] and the second
   face's c[1] - so at rest the pieces tile the wall, and once they
   turn their sides show. Shard_Centre puts the centre, the mean of
   the four, at +0x40 (moving) and +0x50 (rest) and makes the corners
   relative; Shard_Normals gives each of the four faces its normal;
   Shard_RandomSpin gives a random unit axis and a spin of
   (Math_Random() + 0.3)^2 * 0.05 [verified]. Those draws come before Init's
   _srand, from the CRT's stream as everything before part_video::Init
   left it: seed 1, advanced by Tex_CreateTarget's noise fill of the
   four render targets, w * h * 4 draws each at w/4 x h/4, w/2 x h/2
   and twice w x h. So the axes and spins depend on the view size. At
   800 x 450 (the dialog's 800 x 600 with Force 16:9) this gives shard
   0's spin as 0.065890239, the value read from the running demo
   [verified, the live read; the loop at 0x0044e770]. src/rupture
   makes the same draws in the same order, so its shards are the
   demo's at the same view size.

   The launch, in part_video::Init after _srand(0xfcdd0218), per
   shard [verified, 0x004b1447..0x004b18d5]:

    Math_Random()                          drawn and discarded
    d = rest - (0, 57, -16.4) - (80, 60, -70)
    v = normalize(d) * 250 + (r3, r2, r1) * 120 + (0, -150, 0)
                                           rN = 2 Math_Random() - 1,
                                           in draw order
    if rest.x < -120:  v = (20 Math_Random() + 200, 0, 70)
    a = (0, 0, -90)

   so the pieces fly out from one point behind the wall, and those on
   the far left are thrown sideways instead.

   THE FLIGHT IS COMPUTED ONCE, AT LOAD. Obj_BakeShards(12, 20) steps
   every shard 240 times and records it: dt = 0.5 / 20, two
   Shard_Step(dt) per record, and the first record written 11 times
   so the path holds still at its start - 250 records, 0.05 s of
   flight apart [verified]. A step:

    a  += (0, -2, 0) dt
    v  += a dt
    v, a *= max(1 - 0.45 dt, 0)
    pos += v dt
    angle += |v| * spin * 0.3 * dt         while pos.y >= -11999.95

   with a floor at y = -12000 that bounces (v.y * -0.59, v.x and v.z
   * 0.89) and that nothing here reaches [verified, 0x00451210].
   Shard_Record appends pos to one track and (angle, 0, 0) to another.

   Each frame, Shard_Seek(s) reads the record back at a fractional
   index s with Track_Eval, a Catmull-Rom through the records,
   clamped to the ends, then turns the corners and normals by
   Mat_FromEuler(angle * axis), which is Rx(a) Ry(b) Rz(c) with a, b
   and c the angle times the axis's x, y and z [verified]. The draw
   is one glBegin(GL_TRIANGLES) under GL_TEXTURE = translate(0, 57,
   -16.4) and glColor3f(0.4, 0.7, 0.9), with the room pair still
   bound, skipping any shard whose s is over 110. Each shard is four
   triangles, V[k] the turned corner k plus pos [verified,
   0x004afa3b..0x004b01ad]:

    N0: V0 V1 V2    N1: V0 V1 V3    N2: V0 V2 V3    N3: V1 V2 V3

   N0 and N3 are the normalised edge crosses negated, so each normal
   faces out of its face [verified, Shard_Normals].

   s is the choreography: each shard's own delay from t and the
   distances of its REST centre to five points. With e = rest - (0,
   57, -16.4) and e1 = e - (-127, -66.5, 27) [verified,
   0x004af210..0x004afa03]:

    q  = |e1|                              * 0.4 * 0.2 * 0.5 * 4.4
    m1 = |e - (90, -60, 60)|               * 0.4 * 0.1
    m2 = |e - (117, -100, 3)|              * 0.4 * 0.1 * 1.4
    m3 = |rest - (0, 57, 16.4) - (-3, -70, 0)| * 0.4 * 0.1
    m4 = |(-28, -49, 27) - e1|             * 0.4 * 0.1

    m  = min(m1, m2), or 1e8 if m3 * 1.48 < m
    A  = min(max(t + 11 - min(m * 2.2 * 0.2, q), 0) * 20 * 0.02, 10.05)
    B  = max(t - 4 - 4 m, 0) * 20 * 0.18
    C  = (t - 24) * 15 * clamp((t - 24) * 0.35) / (m4^3 + 0.1)
    s  = Math_Ease(A / 10.05, 2) * 10.05 + B + C

   The 20 is k_dbl_20, picked by a test of FUN_00401590(u) * 0 + 1
   against it, with u = max((t - 18) * 0.35, 0); the product is
   always 1 and that test always picks 20, so u has no effect
   [verified]. A shard near the reference points goes first; the far
   ones wait. A brings every shard to s = 10.05, still inside the 11
   identical first records, so nothing moves until B or C does: B from
   t = 4 + 4m, C from t = 24 [inferred, the formulas].

   src/gfx_test shows it: walls.obj's groups as shards, launched,
   baked and replayed with this s on a clock of its own. At rest the
   shards are the whole room, the holed wall included, and from t = 4
   it comes apart [observed, gfx_test].

4.  Textures laid by world position

   Every lookup below uses oThePos, the vertex position after
   ModelViewObject - the world position - not the mesh's texture
   coordinates [verified, the shader files]. A texture laid this way
   stays where it is in the world while a mesh moves, turns or
   scales through it: the mesh is drawn across a fixed sheet.

    TV, 2hh     cc = tex2D(decal, world.xz * 0.016 * (0.8, 1)
                       + (dist, 0) + 0.5 + (0, -0.1)).x
                colour += cc * (1, 1.4 - 20 dist, 1.9) * 0.36
                dist = a product of four sines of 1.4 world.z and
                       ttime, cubed, * 0.04 * multip
                decal = the current video frame

   The picture on the TV is the video laid flat over world x and z
   and rippled by dist; the hex cells only show where it falls.

    room, 2box  discard where 400 * pow(tex2D(decal, world.xz
                * 0.00003).y * tex2D(decal, world.xy * 0.00005).y, 6)
                > ttime
                discard where world.y > 5 and sin(0.07 world.z)
                * cos(0.07 world.x + 0.1 world.y) < -0.3
                decal = textures/bg08.jpg

   The first is a dissolve: ttime rising lets more of each surface
   survive, ttime falling after t = 22 eats it away, and where it
   eats is fixed in the world by bg08's bright patches. The second
   cuts a lattice of holes through anything above y = 5: the wall of
   oval holes is that lattice, not modelled geometry.

   The motor part's road does the same with its blue speckle:
   mul1 = bg08(world.xz * 0.03).x * bg08(world.xy * 0.03).y, raised
   to the 6th power, so it shows only where both lookups are bright
   (effects/motor.txt 2.2).

   The samplers come from maybe_Mesh_Draw: decal and light from
   maybe_Scene_DrawLit's arguments, texture and texture2 from the
   material of the mesh it draws [verified]. On the TV pair decal is
   the video frame, light bg08, texture2 meshes/marmaro.jpg; on the
   room pair decal and light are both bg08 [observed, the shim agent
   at t = 10.29].

5.  Frame and the post chain

   part_video::Frame runs while started[0] and not done[0]
   (subsystems/parts.txt 2). Its clock t (this+0x1b8) starts at 0 in
   Init and gains the frame delta; from t = 29 the shared delta itself
   is first multiplied by 1.1, so the part's last ten seconds run
   fast [verified, 0x004f6120, 0x004f4648]. At t = 39 it hands over.
   Every Frame of a pass takes the same delta, so in the frame of the
   hand-over part_motor's first Frame runs on the scaled delta too, and
   part_motor's clock starts 1.1 frames past its -35 rather than one
   [verified, the shared argument; observed, the agent's t]. The near
   plane is 3.3's.

   5.1.  The camera

   Three Camera_Set calls write the eye camera in a row; the first two
   read seq+0 and seq+8 (copied to this+0x348; always 0, see
   subsystems/parts.txt 3) and are overwritten unseen. The third, and
   a second camera made with Camera_Init, are blended by Camera_Lerp
   [verified, 0x004b1b54..0x004b1fb6]:

    v   = 10 t
    A   eye (320 - v, 1.5 v + 120, 0)   target (0, 0, -50)
        up (0, -0.2, 1)
    T2  = t - Math_SoftRamp(max(t - 27, 0) * 0.5)
    E   = t >= 29 ? 20 Math_Ease(clamp((t - 29) * 0.3), 2) : 0
    q   = 2 - (t - 7 + E) * 0.15
    e2  = Math_Ease(clamp((t - 22) * 0.15), 1)
    B   eye (50 q, 390 - 9 (T2 - 4), -19.5 e2)   target (0, 0, 0)
        up (0 sin T2, 0.1, 1)
    cam = A + (B - A) Math_Ease(clamp((t - 5) * 0.1), 1)

   Math_SoftRamp(u) is pow(u / 2, 2.2) blending into u as 0.3 u goes
   from 0 to 1 [verified, 0x00401590]. The camera slides from A
   toward B over t = 5 .. 15 while both move; after t = 29 the eye
   pulls back along x.

   5.2.  The shake

   Init loads meshes/try1.sav into a path at this+0x20 (Path_Load):
   950 records of float x, y, z and two doubles, the last pushed
   twice by an feof loop, so 951 entries. Each frame [verified,
   0x004b1fbb..0x004b22af]:

    F   = normalize(target - eye),  R = F x up
    i   = trunc(35 t) % 950
    w   = 3 sin(pi clamp((t - 29.5) * 0.3))
    a   = 0.011 + 0.6 w * 0.011,  b = (w + 1) a
    target += R x_i b + up y_i b
    RightV = x_i * 1.1 a * 0.01 * 0.8,  UpV likewise with y_i

   so the camera's aim wanders by the recorded hand-held path at 35
   records a second, and the same values steer bend3's directional
   blur. After t = 29.5 w swells the wander up to four times. A term
   adding sin(...) * 0.5 * 0 to up.x, and the smoothed tracks
   Path_Load builds, change nothing [verified]. All of 5.1 and 5.2
   reproduce the camera, light and RightV/UpV read at clock 10.29 (6)
   to float precision [observed].

   The light camera is the eye camera with its eye moved by
   30 * -14 = -420 in x (0x004f47dc, 0x004f4f74).

   5.3.  The post chain

   DrawScene draws into target 0, then the chain of effects/look.txt
   3: blur (this+0x19c), the edge pass (this+0x1a8), blur six times,
   bend3 (this+0x1a0), then target 0's texture added to the window in
   white. The half- and quarter-size textures are bound first, the
   second with glColor3f 0.15 (0x004f3760), but each bind and colour
   is replaced before the one quad is drawn [verified]. bend3's
   alpha is

    (1 - Math_Ease(clamp((t - 2) * 0.4), 1)) * 0.5 + 0.15
    + 0.6 Math_SoftRamp(max((t - 35) * 0.5, 0))

   [verified, 0x004b239d..0x004b24b6].

   Frame brackets everything in glPushAttrib(GL_ALL_ATTRIB_BITS) and
   glPopAttrib, so each frame starts with Demo_InitGL's depth test
   (GL_LEQUAL) and the matrix mode it began in, although Frame turns
   depth testing off after DrawScene [verified]. Its matrix pushes
   balance only if that mode is GL_PROJECTION: Frame's first push then
   lands on the projection stack, where the sky's stray pop takes it
   off, and the sky's unpopped modelview push is undone by Frame's
   closing pops. Started in GL_MODELVIEW, the modelview stack would
   fill within 32 frames and maybe_Scene_DrawLit would hand the TV a
   stale view; the demo's TV plays throughout, so the mode is
   GL_PROJECTION [inferred; which Init leaves it so is unread].
   src/rupture reproduces the demo's frames at clock 10.29 and 17
   [observed, side by side], and at every 6 s checkpoint from t = 3 to
   39 at the 800x450 view matches the original to a mean of 0.21 per
   8-bit channel or less [observed, the agent pipe on both].
