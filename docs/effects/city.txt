PCDEMOS RUPTURE                                            Analysis Note


                         PART_CITY: THE CITY


Abstract

   part_city is slot 5 of the relay. It runs from t = 0 to t = 59 on
   its own clock and hands over to part_end. Its Init loads meshes
   named for a city, a helicopter and their wrecks, two of them broken
   into shards; its Frame keys nine camera windows on t over
   part_boat's post chain. DrawScene draws a sun and glows, the city
   and its water, five tablets, and from t = 32 the city breaking into
   shards; then the sections of 4.2.

1.  What it draws

   Init loads boat.obj, helicopter.obj, hellicrash2.obj, helix.obj,
   Hellidist.obj, city1.obj, city_less.obj, citywater.obj,
   citycrash.obj and tablet.obj [verified, part_city::Init]. Which of
   them DrawScene draws, and how, is section 4.

2.  Init

   part_city::Init (0x00476630) is part_boat::Init's shape in the slot
   band 0: texBase (this+0x3b8) and model (this+0x1394) are 0. The
   render targets are made only with seq->relayMode 0; pattern5,
   bg08, blob41b (through Tex_LoadWithAlpha, effects/boat.txt 5) and
   cloudt2 go to texture slots 10, 0x5a, 0x14 and 0x1e [verified].
   box.3ds is loaded through maybe_Shader_LoadPairWithModel with the
   first pair, as in the other parts. The pairs:

    +0x13bc  2     vertex_projection_anisotropic
                   fragment_projection_anisotropic2h
    +0x13c4  0x30  vertex_boat   fragment_boat
    +0x13c8  0x2a  vertex_boat2  fragment_city
    +0x318   0x56  vertex_boat2  fragment_city2
    +0x13cc  0x26  vertex_boat   fragment_heli
    +0x13d0  0x22  vertex_boat   fragment_train

   and the post passes blur256 (+0x304, 0x17), blur256FOV (+0x30c,
   0x1d), Blur_circular (+0x300, 0x15), bend3 (+0x308, 0x1f) and
   wireframe (+0x310, 0x1b) [verified]. +0x13c0 (0x20) and +0x314
   (0x19) are set and never loaded. try1.sav goes into the Path at
   this+0x20. Both clocks start at 0 [verified].

   2.1.  The shards

   srand(0x2861f2e) and 106 rand() calls, then citycrash.obj is
   broken into shards with spin 0.12 and Hellidist.obj with spin 5.0
   (Obj_MakeShards, effects/video.txt 3.4). Each shard then gets its
   own throw, with r a fresh Math_Random each time [verified]:

    D = r^3 * 6,  P = (1.2 r)^17 + 2

    citycrash   v = (2r - 1, 5r - 3, 4P) * 0.5 * 10     a = (0, -66, 0)
    Hellidist   v = (4r - 2 - D, 9r - 2, 8r - 4) * 0.2   a = (10, -6, 0)

   Before that each loop builds (rest - (-3.71, 1.81, 0)) P + (0, 3, 0)
   and then (4r - 2 - D, 9r - 2, 8r - 4), and overwrites both before
   storing; the randoms they draw still move the stream [verified].
   Each mesh is then baked with Obj_BakeShards(6, 20).

3.  Frame

   3.1.  The clock

   part_city::Frame (0x00477450) gates on started[5]/done[5].
   maybe_Seq_Copy copies the sequence record into this+0x5a8, so the
   head is h0, h1, h2 at this+0x5a8..0x5b8 and this+0xe60 is the
   previous frame's partClock (effects/boat.txt 3.1). t (this+0x328)
   is tLocal (this+0x330) + g_partTimeCarry, h0 * 5 when seq+0x28 is
   set, and held at -0.0 (0x004f5010) at least [verified].

   nearZ is h2 * 10 + 1.5, 29.5 for 32 <= t < 42, then + 0.4, - 0.3
   for 15 <= t < 22 and + 6 from t = 32; farZ is 2300 [verified]. At
   t = 59 (0x004f52a8) the overshoot goes to g_partTimeCarry and
   part_end starts [verified].

   3.2.  The cameras

   One base camera from the seq head, eye (sin h0, h1 128 / r,
   cos h0) with r = 80 h2 + 10, then windows tested on t [verified]:

    -10 .. 3    eye at radius 5 round the y axis at height
                1336 - 94 t, target (0, 1340 - 105 t, -t)
    3 .. 5      eye at radius 80 - 5 (t - 3) round (129, 285, -73),
                target (129, 269, -90)
    5 .. 15     radius 10 round (129, 275.8, -73), then Camera_Lerp
                to a camera at (92, 420, 20 (t - 5) - 70) by
                Ease(Clamp01((t - 5) 0.08), 2)
    15 .. 22    radius 6 round (-10, 96.8 + 0.8 (t - 15) + 2.4,
                50 (t - 15) - 149), target (-10, 101.8, the same z),
                up (0, 1, -0.4)
    22 .. 32    the same round z = 50 (t - 15) - 963 at height 99.97,
                eased down 1.6 over t = 28..32, up (0, 1, 0.6)
    32 .. 36    (-353, 255, -152) + ((-96, 150, -408) - that) times
                1.7 + 0.05 (t - 32), plus (22, 50, 56 (t - 32)),
                target (-96, 150, -408)
    36 .. 42    radius 490 round (0, 150, 0) at height 260
    42 .. 45    radius 140 round (26, 45, 150)
    45 .. 145   eye (276, 155, 20 (t - 45) - 150), z plus
                10 SoftRamp((t - 47) / 2) from t = 47, target
                (176, 155, z + 60); from t = 54 the eye is raised 12
                and the eye z and target x move 60

   The sums are the demo's Vec4 operators (Vec4_OpAdd 0x004013a0,
   Vec4_OpSub, Vec4_OpScale), float component by component; the
   exact expressions are in the source [verified].

   3.3.  The shake and the light camera

   part_boat's shake on try1.sav: the path index trunc(35 t) mod n,
   trunc(35 (t + 3)) from t = 54. The depth is 0.008, 0.08 from t > 34,
   halved from 36, times 5 for 42 <= t < 45 and times 1 + 2
   Clamp01(t - 49) from 49; the up tilt is sin(1.71887 a depth) 0.05
   times 1, 0 from t = 36 and 1.2 for 42..45. RightV and UpV scale
   with 1.6, 0.224 from t > 34 [verified].

   The light camera is the eye camera with its eye moved by
   (30, 0, 0) * -14 [verified].

   3.4.  The post chain

   part_boat's: blur256 to the half target, wireframe, blur256 six
   times, bend3 and the composite. bend3's alpha is
   (1 - Ease(Clamp01(t - 0.1), 1)) 0.4 + 0.15; RightV and UpV double
   from t = 54. The composite's quarter layer is grey 0.15, and the
   main layer fades in over t = 0.1..1.1 and out over 51..53, then
   to 0.35 over 54..55 and out over 56..58 [verified]. Unlike
   part_boat there is no ttime.

4.  DrawScene

   part_city::DrawScene (0x00471a30) does not read its pass argument.
   Its lit sections are part_boat's bracket (effects/boat.txt 4): no
   push and pop round maybe_Scene_DrawLit, ctx+0x1918, +0x1920 and
   +0x1930 written, and bg08 (slot texBase + 0x5a) passed as both
   textures; after the binds each turns GL_CULL_FACE on [verified].
   At entry it keeps t >= 22 in a stack byte, read only by the
   sections of 4.2, and sets a stack Vec4 to (1, 0, 0, 0) [verified].

   4.1.  The sky, the city and its fall

   The projection is 55 degrees plus ctx+0x608e38 minus five terms:
   Ease(Clamp01(2.25 (t - 19)), 2) times -0.0, which is always 0;
   30 Ease(Clamp01(0.6 (t - 48.5)), 1); 11 sin(pi Clamp01(0.3 (t -
   9.4))); before t = 32, 50 Clamp01(0.3 (t - 30.5)); from t = 32,
   30 (1 - Clamp01(0.3 (t - 31.5))) [verified]. So the view narrows
   to 44 degrees at t = 11.1 and is back at 55 by t = 12.7; it
   closes to 32.5 over t = 30.5..32, opens again from 29.5 to 55 over
   t = 32..34.8, and narrows to 25 over t = 48.5..50.2. The aniso2h
   pair's planecut is 11.2 and the heli pair's ttime 0.

   The clear colour is k (0.1, 0.13, 0.16) with k = 0.15 Clamp01((t -
   6) / 2) - 0.15 Clamp01((t - 52.5) / 2) [verified].

   The sun is one additive blob41b quad of size 21 in (0.4, 0.3, 0.3),
   and two cloudt2 glows of size 60 in (0.03, 0.05, 0.08) at angles
   -48 and -8, 30 apart in x and wobbling 11 sin(4 k + 0.8 t); all at
   eye + (20, 30, -150) with depth writes off, every t [verified].

   Then the lit sections, each under srand(0x409bfa) and drawn black
   unless named:

    +0x13c8  city      city1.obj before t = 32, city_less.obj after;
                       citywater.obj in (0, 0.3, 0.7)
    +0x318   city2     from t = 3, five tablet.obj copies at fixed
                       places and turns, each in its own bracket
    +0x13c8  city      from t = 32, citycrash.obj's shards

   The city pairs get tick = 2 Clamp01(0.4 t) - 1, and from t = 3
   2 Clamp01(0.1 (t + 0.5)) - 1, and ttime 100 [verified];
   fragment_city2 has neither uniform.

   citycrash's shards are seeked, with culling off, to
   10.8 max(t - 32.5 - 1.1 v, 0), where v is the distance of the
   shard's rest centre from (-121, 109.1, -404.32), divided by 100;
   before t = 36 a shard more than 108 from it in x and z gets v =
   100000 and stays whole; v gains 2.9 from t = 42 and 7 more from 45
   [verified]. The collapse therefore spreads outwards from that point
   at about 91 units a second from t = 32.5. Each shard is drawn as its four
   triangles, part_truck's order (effects/truck.txt); for 45 <= t <
   53.99 the first 100 and the last 200 are skipped, and from t = 45
   one more than sqrt(231) from rest is drawn with GL_CULL_FACE on,
   the rest with it off [verified]. The skip bound is the shard count
   less 300, unsigned, so with fewer than 300 shards it would wrap and
   the loop would run past the list; citycrash.obj has more, so the
   release never does [verified]. The port stops at 0 instead
   (section 5).

   4.2.  The boat, the helicopter and the stars

   From 0x004739c7 five sections follow, the first three lit:

    0x004739c7  +0x13c4 boat   boat.obj before t = 15
    0x00473d7e  +0x13cc heli   before t = 34, the helicopter as its
                               three groups, a bracket each
    0x00474e93  +0x13cc heli   22 <= t < 32, Hellidist.obj's shards
    0x00475e28  no program     1100 stars
    0x00476258  no program     before t = 3, 350 sparks at the boat

   The boat gets ttime 100 and srand(0x409bfa). It is translated to
   (0, 1340 - 110 t, 0), and from t = 9 to (104, 358.6 - 20 u,
   23 u + 27) with u = t - 12.6; then turned -90 degrees about
   (1, 0.23, 0) and -60 t about (0.1, 0, 1), that turn made twice
   from t = 9; drawn in (0.7, 0.7, 0.6) [verified]. The translation
   stays in DrawScene's frame (ESP+0x270) for the sparks.

   The helicopter's ttime is 3 - 1.5 Clamp01(0.06 (t - 19)). Before
   t = 15 it starts at (129, 268.6, -73) and is pulled toward the eye
   by 0.89 Ease(Clamp01(0.2 (t - 6)), 1), its z pushed 2 S(t - 10)
   and 4.4 S(t - 13) and its x 4.4 S(t - 13), S(v) =
   SoftRamp(0.9 max(v, 0)); for 3 <= t < 5 it is turned -114 about y.
   After that it rides the camera windows of 3.2 [verified]:

    15 .. 22   (-10, 96.8 + 4 R, z) with z the window's target z and
               R = SoftRamp(0.5 max(t - 18, 0)); pitched 23 R about
               (1, -3.8, 0), 9 about x, rocked 1.2 sin(3.3 t) about z
    22 .. 32   (-10, 96.8 + 0.8 (1 - Ease(Clamp01(t - 24), 1)), z)
    32 .. 52   (-96, 150 - 20 u, 144 u - 658), u = t - 32

   The last two are turned 12 about x and -4 about z, rocked
   0.36 sin(1.3 t) about z, turned 90 about y and swayed 0.24 sin(2 t)
   about (0.2, 0, 0.8); the first ends with 90 about y and 0.8 sin(4 t)
   about (0.2, 0, 0.8). Every window then tilts 38 Ease(Clamp01(0.29
   (t - 12)), 1) about (1, 0, -0.3) and -42 Ease(Clamp01(0.3 (t -
   9.5)), 1) about x, and before t = 15 -288 Ease(...) about
   (0.3, 1, 0) with the ease of the pull [verified].

   Group 1, the main rotor, spins about y at 420 W degrees and the
   tail rotor about z through (9.07, 2.82, -0.44) at 980 W, W =
   -2 SoftRamp(max(0.8 (t - 5.5), 0)). From t = 22 (the stack byte
   of 4) groups 0 and 2 are hellicrash2.obj's and the tail rotor is
   group 2; group 1 stays helicopter.obj's [verified]. The window's
   position is the stack Vec4 of 4, written by the windows only, so
   before t = 15 it is (0, 0, 0); posx is 0.12 times its z, and after
   the groups it is stored to this+0x13e0, which nothing in DrawScene
   or Frame reads [verified].

   For 22 <= t < 32 the helicopter breaks: Hellidist.obj's shards are
   drawn at that position with the same turns, culling off, under
   srand(0x409bfa). A shard whose rest centre has x = X is seeked to
   min(0.2 max(t - 3 X + 6, 0), 9.7), and when a random r2 has
   X < r2 - 3.4, also to 10.8 max(t - X - 32 - 3 r1, 0) more, r1 a
   random drawn first; from t = 32 it would be 45 less, which the
   window excludes. Each shard is its four triangles, each in its own
   glBegin, part_truck's order [verified].

   The stars are drawn under srand(0xa567) with no program, point
   size 2 and depth testing on, under a 65 / 1 / 1000 projection:
   grey 0.2 (r + 0.2), at 720 along the normalised (2r - 1,
   |2r - 1|, 2r - 1) from the eye, the upper half sphere. An
   Ease(Clamp01(0.3 t), 1) is computed and its result replaced by 1.
   The section's modelview push is popped in GL_PROJECTION mode, so
   DrawScene leaves the modelview stack one deeper [verified].

   Before t = 3 350 additive points of size 1: a random point in the
   cube scaled 0.84, raised 18 f + 2 with f the fraction of
   (0.8 r + 1) (t + 10000) / 2 + 100, scaled 1.7, lowered 10 and moved
   to the boat's translation; coloured (0.1, 0.14, 0.16) times
   Clamp01(0.9 sin(pi f)) [verified].

5.  Reconstruction notes

   src/rupture/demo/part_city.cpp holds the constructor, Frame and
   Init; part_city_draw.cpp holds DrawScene and the sections of 4.1,
   part_city_draw2.cpp those of 4.2, with City_Draw
   (part_city_draw.h) carrying the stack values that cross between
   them. The four Obj meshes at this+0x3c8..0x47c that Init never
   loads, and the std::vector at this+0x638, are left out of the
   port's class. So are this+0x1370, this+0x1374 (0x70), this+0x1380
   (0x6f), this+0x13f0 (200), this+0x13c0 and this+0x314: Init sets
   them and nothing reads them - no instruction in part_city's methods
   uses those offsets but Init's stores and the constructor's, and the
   one other function touching +0x13f0 and +0x314 heavily is part_end's
   DrawScene (0x0047a770), on part_end's own fields [verified, the
   instruction search]. Every part's Init sets a block of slot numbers
   in the same pattern, and part_truck and part_rail keep unused ones
   too, so these look like what copy-and-edit left behind rather than
   developer tooling [inferred].

   One deliberate divergence: citycrash's draw loop stops at 0 where
   the demo's unsigned bound would wrap (section 4.1). It draws the
   same shards with the release's data.

   With the port at 800x450 and both on the agent's fixed step, the
   eye camera at t = 6, 18, 30, 42 and 54 matches the original's at
   g_renderCtx+0x1848 in every float but up.x, which differs by up to
   2e-7: the shake's sin, x87 in the demo [observed].

   At t = 2, 5, 10, 18, 26, 30, 36, 42, 48, 54 and 58 the port's frame,
   cropped to the original's 794x421, differs from the original's in
   at most about 200 pixels by more than 16 of 255, all of them star
   points of 4.2 drawn at the same places with their light spread
   differently over the 2x2 pixels; this+0x13e0 reads (1, 0, 0, 0) in
   both at t = 58 and (1, -10, 96.8, -413) in the port at t = 26
   [observed].

   With only the sections of 4.1 drawn, the port's frames at t = 3,
   33, 40 and 47 differ from the original's by a mean of under 0.5 in
   each 8-bit channel, and at t = 6, 12, 18, 24 and 30 only where the
   helicopter, its shards and the stars of 4.2 are missing; the sky,
   the city, the tablets and citycrash's collapse match [observed].

6.  Open questions

   o The size-2 star points of 4.2 are brighter at their centres in
     the port, up to about twice [observed, t = 18]. Target 0 is
     4-sample multisampled, and legacy GL draws a multisampled point as
     a circle of its size tested per sample, where a GL 3.3 core
     gl_PointSize point is a square, so the original covers fewer
     samples [inferred]. Matching it needs per-sample coverage in the
     fragment stage (gl_SampleMask, GL 4.0 / GLSL ES 3.2), which
     neither GL 3.3 core nor WebGL2 has; scaling the brightness instead
     would be an invented factor. Left as it is.
