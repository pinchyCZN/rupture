PCDEMOS RUPTURE                                            Analysis Note


                       PART_TRUCK: THE TRUCK AND THE TUNNEL


Abstract

   part_truck is slot 2 of the relay. The motorcycle rides on through a
   canyon of broken buildings, overtakes an articulated truck, and the
   camera ends at a tunnel mouth where the truck is cut into shards. It
   runs from t = -8 to t = 66 on its own clock, which is held back near
   the end, then hands over to part_rail.

1.  What it draws

   The road (part_motor's, drawn deeper), the motorcycle until t = 17,
   meshes/truck.obj until t = 59, then truck_empty2.obj's groups and the
   truckdestruction.obj shards flying apart; a cross track and two
   railway tracks from t = 50.4, trainlong.obj, boat.obj, the truck's
   lights, hex2/hex3 cells, a sign and a tholos [verified, the meshes
   each section draws]. It looks like a night road through a canyon
   ending in a tunnel [observed, the original at t = 0..64 in steps of
   8, the shim's fixed-step mode].

2.  Init

   part_truck::Init (0x004aa5e0) is part_motor::Init's shape: the same
   render targets under the same relayMode gate, four textures
   (pattern5, cloudt2, blob41b through maybe_Tex_LoadWithAlpha, bg08),
   twelve meshes into fixed display lists, box.3ds with the
   projection_anisotropic pair through maybe_Shader_LoadPairWithModel,
   twelve shader pairs and the six post passes, meshes/try1.sav, and
   both clocks at -8 [verified]. The pair and file of every slot are
   in its plate comment.

   truckdestruction.obj is cut into shards by Obj_MakeShards(1.0) with
   no srand of its own, so the stream is whatever the render targets'
   noise left. Each shard then takes six Math_Random draws:

    a  = 6 r1^3
    v  = (0, 8 r3 - 3, -(8 r2 + 3a))
    a' = (9 r6, 0 (2 r5 - 1), 8 r4 - 4) * a * 1.6 * 0

   so the acceleration is all (signed) zeros, and Obj_BakeShards(8, 20)
   records the flight [verified]. Seven straight tracks are built: the
   road this+0x1378, 1680 points along +x from (-99, -50, 0); the cross
   track this+0x13d8, 950 points stepping -0.7 in z from (0, -50, 450);
   and five rails this+0x1438 (stride 0x60), 650 points stepping +0.7
   in z from (0, -50, 0) with the step turned in yaw by (j - 2) 0.003
   rad after each add, through Vec4_RotateEuler [verified].

3.  Frame

   3.1.  The clock

   part_truck::Frame (0x004ab200) gates on started[2]/done[2]. tLocal
   (this+0x318) gains the frame delta and is held at -8 at least; the
   part clock t (this+0x310) is tLocal + g_partTimeCarry, less 2 once
   tLocal reaches 63 and less 4 once it reaches 65 [verified]. So t
   pauses twice near the end: in the original, t = 56 to 64 took 12
   seconds of demo clock [observed]. nearZ is 0.05, + 2.1 for
   59 <= t < 63, + 0.5 for t < 63; farZ 550. At t = 66 the overshoot
   goes to g_partTimeCarry and part_rail starts; partEnd is t alone,
   without the carry part_motor adds [verified].

   3.2.  The cameras

   One unconditional camera, then fifteen windows. Their conditions
   test tLocal + carry, not t, and their bodies read tLocal; u = 27 t.
   From the eleventh window (T >= 56) the eye sits at fixed world
   positions near x = 1390 - the tunnel - instead of following u
   [verified]. The expressions are in the source and Frame's plate.

   3.3.  The shake

   As part_motor's, but the path index is trunc(35 t) mod (n - 1) with
   no +300000 offset (trunc(35 t + 245) for 25 <= t < 46), and the
   amplitude A and frequency B come from a chain of time-gated
   overrides, modulated by sin(pi clamp((t - 8.5) 0.7)). bend3's alpha
   is the constant 0.2, and RightV and UpV are the shake's x and y
   times -1.1 A 0.01, scaled up near the end [verified]. The composite
   draws the scene image in white: there is no fade.

4.  DrawScene

   4.1.  The sky and the lamp flag

   part_truck::DrawScene (0x004a2d00) seeds 0xdc072526, then sets the
   byte this+0x2c0 - the truck's lamps - from maybe_Math_Flicker, a
   noisy step that ends on: 1 - (1 - (x - h))^2 with h a sign test of
   sin(24.4 x^2 + C), compared with 0.5 at t - 6, t - 13 and 2 (t - 58)
   [verified]. The stars are motor_sky's loop under another seed; the
   sun and three glows are drawn always, the glows pushed along z
   [verified].

   4.2.  The road and the rails

   The road is maybe_Track_DrawRoadDepth on this+0x1378: part_motor's
   road with the side strips dropped by 1.5 depth, depth = 1 before
   t = 28 and 100 - 95 clamp((t - 31) 0.5) + 95 clamp(t - 46) after;
   it is not drawn for 36 <= t < 39.3 and 43 <= t < 46.6, with the pair
   switched to f_bridge after t = 28 [verified]. From t = 53 the cross
   track and from t = 50.4 two of the five rails are drawn with
   maybe_Track_DrawRails: two rail tops, walls, and sleeper boxes every
   fourth point [verified].

   4.3.  The truck and its shards

   Before t = 59 truck.obj is placed on the road track with a jolt on
   every third 30 Hz frame; from t = 59 truck_empty2.obj's two groups,
   and the truckdestruction shards, each seeked by its distance from
   (-14, 3.1, -1.32) and t and drawn as four triangles in a grey of its
   own [verified, 0x004a446d..0x004a6ec4].

   4.4.  The rest

   The motorcycle and the sparks are part_motor's, before t = 17. Then
   trainlong after t = 53, boat.obj after t = 60.5, the truck lights
   when the lamp flag is set, the hex2 grid reseeded per column, hex3
   ring pieces, the sign, two tholos groups, an additive headlight glow
   with a 30-quad trail after t = 61.4, and from tLocal 63 four bursts
   of 450 points seeded 0xdc05549f. The last glPopMatrix balances the
   push made after the sky [verified].

6.  Reconstruction notes

   src/rupture/demo/part_truck.cpp holds the constructor, Frame and
   Init; part_truck_draw.cpp DrawScene and its helpers. The helpers
   part_motor and part_truck share live in scene.cpp.

   The port matches the original at every 6 s checkpoint from t = -6
   to the part's end at the 800x450 view, to a mean of 0.14 per 8-bit
   channel or less [observed, the agent pipe on both].
