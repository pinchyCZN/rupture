PCDEMOS RUPTURE                                            Analysis Note


                                 MOTOR


Abstract

   part_motor: a motorcycle in a room of hexagonal geometry, drawn
   with the shading that gives RUPTURE its look - surfaces dark where
   they face the camera and glowing where they turn edge-on - then
   blurred and added back for glow. Frame 0x004938a0, Init
   0x00493230. Meshes motorcycle.obj, room.obj, hex2.obj,
   kthrio1.obj.

1.  What it draws

   Init loads meshes/motorcycle.obj, meshes/room.obj, meshes/hex2.obj
   and meshes/kthrio1.obj [verified]. Frame places a hand-keyed
   camera through fourteen time windows (5.2). DrawScene draws a
   starfield, the city of room.obj copies either side of a raised
   road, the bouno1 tile field below, the motorcycle and its sparks
   (4); Frame runs the shared post chain over it (6).

2.  The road blocks' shading

   The pair loaded into this+0x1180 [verified, part_motor::Init]:

    cgeffects/vertex_boat_test2cut.cpp
    cgeffects/fragment_boat_test2.cpp

   shades ten copies of meshes/room.obj (this+0x194), laid end to end
   down x as the road the camera flies along; their dissolving front
   (2.2) is the red strip down its middle [verified, the Obj_Draw at
   0x00491201 takes this+0x194, which Init loads from room.obj;
   observed, 2.4]. The motorcycle (g_motorcycleObj) is a different
   draw, the Obj_Draw at 0x004921fb, with the pair in this+0x11e8,
   v_motor and f_motor [verified], section 3.

   2.1.  The rim term

   The vertex program computes, per vertex [verified, the file]:

    V    = normalize(CameraPos - P)
    m    = max(dot(N, V), 0)
    mm   = pow(m, 15) * 2
    m    = pow(1 - m, 5) + 0.5
    mult = 1 / (1 + 0.0005 * |CameraPos - P|^2)

    HCOL2 = diffuse * m  * mult * 0.65
    HCOL  = diffuse * mm * mult * 3

   with N and P transformed by ModelViewObject. m is a Fresnel
   rim term: 0.5 where the surface faces the camera, rising to 1.5 as
   it turns edge-on, so silhouettes come out up to three times
   brighter than faces seen head-on. The crease lines are not this
   term's work but the post chain's (effects/look.txt 3.2). HCOL is a
   narrow head-on highlight. mult fades both with the square of
   distance.

   diffuse is the current glColor. part_motor::DrawScene draws the
   blocks with glColor4f(0.2, 0.22, 0.2, 1) [verified, floats at
   0x004f3754 and 0x004f5de8].

   The vertex program also widens the mesh along z by up to 2 units,
   driven by the uniform Reduction.x against the vertex's x
   [verified]:

    ffx = saturate(0.05 * (Reduction.x - x))
    z  += (z > 0 ? 2 : -2) * ffx

   Its earlier assignments to HCOL and HCOL2 are overwritten before
   the return; only the last ones above take effect [verified].

   2.2.  The fragment program

   Two outputs [verified, the file]:

    oColor1 = (N + 1) * 0.5                       COLOR0: the normal
    oColor2 = HCOL2 * (1 + (0.2,0.6,1,1) * 4000
                         * saturate(mul1^6 - 0.001))
            + saturate(ffx - cond2) * 2 * (1,0.2,0.1,1)
                                   * saturate(1 - diff)
            + HCOL * 0.4                          COLOR1: the colour
    oColor2.w = Ramp.x

   mul1 is the product of two lookups into the decal texture at
   world-space positions (xz and xy, scaled by 0.03): a procedural
   mask that pushes a strong blue into the colour where it peaks.

   Three discards cut the mesh [verified]:

    cos(P0.y * 0.7) * cos(P0.z * 0.7) < -0.7      a regular lattice of
                                                   holes, P0 = object
                                                   space position
    cond1 > 0.97                                   the noise mask plus
                                                   alpha2 and height
    y > -25, x < alphaplane2,
    cond2 < 0.73 * ffx                             a dissolving front
                                                   moving in x

   2.3.  What the blocks are fed

   Obj_Draw sets no texture and no sampler, and nothing in the loop
   sets the
   fragment program's decal. The sampler reads whatever is bound, and
   at every motor Obj_Draw that was GL texture 853 on unit 0 - 512 x
   512, internal format 3 - with units 1 to 3 empty [observed, the
   shim agent at 76 s and 88 s]. Its pixels are textures/bg08.jpg, the
   texture Init loads into this[0x2a0]+0xc at 0x0049346a [observed,
   TEXDUMP against the file]. That decal is mul1's source, so bg08
   drives the blue mask, cond1 and cond2. Which earlier draw leaves it
   bound is open; maybe_Mesh_Draw is the only code that sets texture
   parameters on scene programs [verified, cross-references to
   cgGLSetTextureParameter].

   The uniforms, all through Shader_SetParam3f and Shader_SetParam1f,
   the only callers of cgGLSetParameter3f and 1f [verified]:

    CameraPos    vertex   index 1   ctx+0x184c, the eye camera
    LightPos     vertex   index 2   (0, 0, 30)
    Reduction    vertex   index 4   (r, 0, 0), set per copy (2.4)
    alpha2, ttime, alphaplane,
    alphaplane2  fragment           set per copy (2.4)

   LightPos is the light camera ctx+0x18a8: Frame copies a camera
   into it and then writes its position as (0, 0, 30.0f), the float
   at 0x004f47dc, before calling DrawScene [verified, 0x00494f87;
   observed constant through the motor part]. In this pair it reaches
   only assignments the program overwrites (2.1), so it does not
   change the colour. maybe_Scene_DrawLit sets ModelViewProj from the
   eye camera and ModelViewLightProj from the light camera
   (translate and scale by 0.5, k_flt_0_5, then gluPerspective of
   ctx+0x608e38 + 75 at aspect ctx+0x608e50, then its gluLookAt)
   [verified]; the light projection reaches only the dead cll1 and
   cll4 lookups.

   Sampled values [observed, the shim agent; two to five frames per
   sample, the first copy's]:

   +-------+-------------+--------+-------+-------------+--------------+
   | clock | alphaplane2 | alpha2 | ttime | Reduction.x | CameraPos    |

   | 70.0  | -74.1       | 0      | 0     | -79.2       | -164 -21 0   |

   alphaplane is 0 throughout. Reduction.x tracks alphaplane2 about 5

   2.4.  Where the blocks are

   The loop runs while the part's clock t (this+0x210) is above -5
   (k_dbl_m5) [verified, 0x00490e19 onward]. With L = 27 t and E the
   eye camera's x (ctx+0x184c), plus 91 once t >= 9:

    n = clamp(__ftol2(E / 24 + 0), 5, 15)
    for i = n - 5 .. n + 4:
      _srand(0x409bfa + i), then 100 Math_Random calls discarded
      d     = L - 24 i
      ttime = Math_Ease(clamp((10 + d + 20) * 0.015), 2)
      turn  = Math_Ease(clamp((d + 100) * 0.015), 2)
      lift  = clamp((d - 30 + 0) * 0.005)
      GL_TEXTURE = translate(24 i, (i % 2) * 3 - 20.4, 0)
                   rotate((Math_Random() * 40 - 20) * turn + 90, y)
      alpha2      = Math_Random() * ttime
      alphaplane  = (L - 150 - 4) * 0
      alphaplane2 = L + 10 - 4
      translate(0, lift * 0, 0)
      Reduction   = (L + 10 - 10, 0, 0)

   The constants are labelled at their addresses (k_dbl_27, k_dbl_24,
   k_dbl_91, k_dbl_9, k_dbl_10, k_dbl_20, k_dbl_0_015, k_dbl_100,
   k_dbl_30, k_dbl_0_005, k_dbl_150, k_dbl_4, k_dbl_20_4, k_dbl_40,
   k_dbl_90, k_dbl_2, k_dbl_0); Math_Random is _rand() / 32767. The
   multiplies by k_dbl_0 make alphaplane 0 and the lift nothing. The
   GL_TEXTURE matrix becomes ModelViewObject, so each block's world
   position is its translate [verified; observed, the shim agent's
   mtex operands matched this to the last digit at 78.2 s, with
   Math_Ease's inputs and outputs hooked].

   One instant read from the demo, paused at clock 78.30 [observed,
   the shim agent]:

    t            5.247628168765237      part_motor this+0x210
    eye          106.238144 62.026756 2.026756    ctx+0x184c
    target       66.244392 -21.068668 -0.123311   ctx+0x185c
    up           -0.102871 1 0                    ctx+0x186c
    light cam    eye (0, 0, 30), target 0, up (0, 1, 0)
    ctx+0x608e38 0 (fov offset), +0x608e40 0.35, +0x608e48 550,
                 +0x608e50 1 (the light's aspect)
    bend3        RightV 0.00701752452, UpV -0.0266499047

   So DrawScene's projection is gluPerspective(65, the window's
   aspect, 0.35, 550). At that instant n = 5 and the blocks stand 70
   to 410 units from the eye, so mult runs from 0.29 down to 0.01;
   across clock 68 to 96 the nearest block is 70 to 110 units away
   [observed]. With mult = 1 / (1 + 0.0005 d^2) and Ramp = mult * 0.5,
   and the edge pass multiplying its whole output by Ramp
   (effects/look.txt 3.2), that distance sets the brightness of the
   look as much as any colour does [inferred, the formulas].

3.  The motorcycle

   The bike is g_motorcycleObj (meshes/motorcycle.obj) drawn once
   per frame with the pair in this+0x11e8, v_motor and f_motor. It
   rides a track that Init builds, the same Track_Eval type the
   shards replay (effects/video.txt 3.4).

   3.1.  The road track

   Init empties the track this+0x1188 with Track_Clear and pushes
   1350 points onto it [verified, 0x00493554..0x004936df]. A point
   starts at (-100, -50, 0); before each push its y is assigned and
   (1, 0, 0) is added, so point i sits at x = i - 99:

    i = 0 .. 749      y = Math_Ease(clamp(i / 250, 0, 1), 2) * 150 - 150
    i = 750 .. 1349   j = i - 750
                      y = (1 - Math_Ease(clamp(j / 250, 0, 1), 2)) * 50 - 50

   Math_Ease(x, 2) runs 0 to 1, so the road climbs from -150 to 0
   over its first 250 points, runs level to point 749, drops to -50
   over the next 250 and is level after that. z is 0 throughout. The
   second loop draws one Math_Random per point and drops it, which
   moves the rand stream by 600 [verified].

   3.2.  The transform

   With t the part's clock this+0x210, DrawScene keeps
   u = t * 27 [verified, k_dbl_27 at 0x00490872], less 3.2 around this
   draw while t < 14. The bike is placed by [verified, 0x00491b00 ..
   0x00492200]:

    s  = max(u + 245 + 5, 5)
    P  = Track_Eval(this+0x1188, (float)s)
    Q  = Track_Eval(this+0x1188, (float)(s + 3))
    D  = normalize(Q - P)
    a  = Math_Ease(clamp((t + 9.4) * 0.3, 0, 1), 1)
    b  = Math_Ease(clamp((t + 9.8) * 0.3, 0, 1), 1)
    k  = clamp(sin(a * pi) * 1.4, 0, 1)
    yaw = acos(dot(D, (1, 0, 0))) * -90

    glMatrixMode(GL_TEXTURE); glLoadIdentity()
    glTranslatef(P.x, P.y, P.z)
    glTranslatef(-180, -20, 0)
    t < -2:  glTranslatef(sin(b * pi) * 2.8 * 2, 0, 0)
    glTranslatef(0, yaw * -4.2 * 0.01, 0)
    t < -2:  glTranslatef(0, k * 1.664, 0)
    t > 0:   glRotatef(sin(t * 2) * 20, 1, 0, 0)
    glRotatef(90, 0, 1, 0)
    glRotatef(yaw, 1, 0, 0)
    t < -2:  glRotatef(k * -50, 1, 0, 0)
    glScalef(1, 1, 1); glColor3f(0.3, 0.3, 0.3)
    Shader_SetTextureMatrix(this+0x11e8, 0xe)
    glMatrixMode(GL_MODELVIEW); Obj_Draw(g_motorcycleObj)

   Before it: maybe_Scene_DrawLit with this pair, both programs bound,
   GL_CULL_FACE on, _srand(0x409bfa), and alphaplane2 = u - 150 - 4
   on the fragment program, which f_motor declares and never reads.
   The code also forms R = (0, 1, 0) x D and R x D, normalises both,
   and reads neither [verified].

   The bike stands at s = 5 until u + 250 passes 5, at t = -8.96, and
   then moves at 27 units a second. The eases a and b start at t =
   -9.4 and -9.8 and reach 1 3.3 s later; sin(a pi) and sin(b pi)
   rise and fall back to 0 over that time, so k raises the bike by up
   to 1.664 and turns it about x by up to -50 degrees while b moves
   it by up to 5.6 in x, all before t = -2 [inferred, the formulas].
   On screen that should be a wheelie as it sets off [guess]. acos
   discards the sign of the slope, so the bike pitches the same way
   up the climb and down the drop, by 90 per radian rather than 57.3
   [inferred].

   3.3.  v_motor and f_motor

   v_motor computes [verified, the file]:

    P    = ModelViewObject * position          oThePos
    N    = (float3x3)ModelViewObject * normal  oNormal
    V    = normalize(CameraPos - P)
    m    = max(dot(N, V), 0)
    mm   = pow(m, 15) * 2
    m    = pow(1 - m, 5) + 0.5
    mult = 1 / (1 + 0.001 * |CameraPos - P|^2)

    HCOL2 = diffuse * m  * mult * 0.5
    HCOL  = diffuse * mm * mult
    Ramp  = (1, 1, 1) * mult

   The rim term of 2.1 with a falloff twice as steep and Ramp without
   the halving. Its spherical-map, specular, diffuse and tangent-frame
   work is overwritten or reaches no output f_motor reads.

   f_motor [verified, the file]:

    mul1    = tex2D(decal, oThePos.xz * 0.08).x
            * tex2D(decal, oThePos.xy * 0.09).y
    oColor1 = (oNormal.xyzz + 1) * 0.5                  COLOR0
    oColor2 = HCOL2 * (0.2, 0.3, 0.4, 0.1) * 0.2
            * (1 + (0.3, 0.3, 0.4, 1) * 100000 * Ramp.x
                 * saturate(pow(mul1, 8)))              COLOR1
    oColor2.w = Ramp.x

   No discard is live and HCOL is unused. The body is dark, a fifth of
   0.3 * m * mult tinted blue, and where mul1 approaches 1 the factor
   100000 * mul1^8 throws it to white, a speckle fixed in world space
   that the bike moves through [inferred]. Which texture is decal is
   2.3's question: nothing in this draw binds one.

4.  The rest of DrawScene

   DrawScene's order [verified, 0x00490100..0x0049322d]: the sky into
   the cleared target; the scene projection gluPerspective(65 +
   ctx+0x608e38, aspect, near 0.35, far 550); the near buildings, the
   road blocks (2), the far buildings, the road surface, the tile
   field, the motorcycle (3), kthrio1 before t = -5, and the sparks.
   Every pair is set up by the same bracket: profiles off, ctx+0x1bd8
   = 0, maybe_Scene_DrawLit(model 1000, decal = texBase + 12 (bg08),
   light = texture 0 (the normal target), the pair, the eye and light
   cameras), ctx+0x1bd8 = 1, the pair bound. DrawScene keeps its own
   u = 27 t (Frame's is 25 t), and a row count n = clamp(trunc(eye.x
   / 24), 5, 15), from eye.x + 91 after t = 9. Each building or block
   reseeds the C runtime's generator from its row index and draws 100
   values before its own, so its look is fixed per index.

   4.1.  The sky

   gluPerspective(65, aspect, 1, 1000) and the eye camera, depth test
   off, _srand(0x193cedbf), and 2500 GL_POINTS of grey 0.2 (r + 0.2)
   on the upper half of a sphere of radius 400, its y made positive,
   lowered by 160 and following the eye in x. Before t = 13 a blob41b
   sun 35 across and two cloudt2 glows 110 across beside it, offset
   in x by sin(0.8 t + 4k) * 11 + 30 k, all additive [verified,
   0x00490100..0x004906e0].

   4.2.  The buildings

   room.obj again, with the pair this+0x11ec (fragment_boat_test3). The
   near row: indices n - 4 (n - 5 for 9 <= t < 14) to n + 3, at
   (24 i + 12, 3 (i % 2) - 20.4 - 10 r - 10, -30), turned three times
   by (20 r - 10) Math_Ease(clamp((u - 24 i + 50) * 0.015), 2) degrees,
   tinted (0.8, 0.9 + 0.1 r, 0.9 + 0.3 r). The far row, after t = -5:
   indices n - 3 (n - 4) to n + 2 but every fifth, at z = +30, tinted
   (0.8 + 0.2 r, 1, 1). Each sets alpha2 = r on the fragment program.
   They tip into place as the road reaches them [verified,
   0x004908eb..0x00490d7a, 0x0049122a..0x004915ba].

   4.3.  The road surface

   The pair this+0x1184 (v_roadcut, f_roadcut) under translate(-180,
   -20.06, 0), colour (0.6, 0.8, 1), Reduction ((u - 30) - 10, 0, 0),
   alphaplane2 u - 24, draws the road track with maybe_Track_DrawRoad:
   a top quad strip 8 wide, two sides 1.5 deep and centre dashes, all
   from the track's points with no smoothing beyond the 1-unit
   spacing. It clears the track's loop byte, which the bike's
   Track_Eval then sees [verified, 0x004915c0..0x0049184a,
   0x0048ec00].

   4.4.  The tile field

   After t = -8.5, the pair this+0x11f8 (fragment_boat_testhex) draws
   textures/bouno1.png, a 70 x 70 image read as 8-bit grey, as a field
   of separate square tiles, one per pixel, 0.03 high per grey level,
   each shrunk 3% toward its centre and scaled by 7.5: once at
   (50, -110, -50) before t = 9 and once turned 180 about y at (450,
   -110, 160) (and z - 110 for 9 <= t < 14) after; doit = 0 and 1
   [verified, 0x00491850..0x00491ae9, 0x0048dfc0].

   4.5.  kthrio1

   Before t = -5, meshes/kthrio1.obj with the pair this+0x112c at
   translate(-282.5, -160.59, 2.1) rotate(90, y) scale(5), colour
   (0.8, 0.9, 1), alphaplane2 = 1 - Math_Ease(clamp((t + 35) * 0.4),
   1), so it dissolves away over the part's first 2.5 s [verified,
   0x0049222d..0x00492481].

   4.6.  The sparks

   _srand(0x409bfa), then up to 600 untextured, unblended billboards,
   600 clamp((t + 8.96) * 2.6) of them, from the road 2 behind the
   bike's place on the track, raised 2 and 180 back in x. Each has its
   own phase v = clamp(frac(500 r1 + 0.6 (r2 + 1) t) * c): it flies
   back 30 v along the road, spreads 6 v r sideways and up, falls by
   r + 12.4 v^2, shrinks as 0.04 (1.5 (1 - v))^2 and fades as (0.2,
   0.05, 0.01) (3 (1 - v))^4, which GL clamps to white-orange while v is small
   [verified, 0x00492542..0x0049314b]. A last gluPerspective(65, ...,
   1, 1000), an empty GL_POINTS and a glPopMatrix close DrawScene; the
   pop takes the push made after the sky.

5.  Frame

   5.1.  The clock

   part_motor::Frame runs while started[1] and not done[1]. Its local
   clock this+0x218 starts at -35 in Init, gains the frame delta, is
   held at -35.01 at least, and t = local + g_partTimeCarry; at t = 29
   it hands over to part_truck. near 0.35, far 550 [verified,
   0x004938b1..0x00493a26].

   5.2.  The cameras

   u = 25 t, or -275 before t = -5. Fourteen Camera_Set calls, each
   behind a time window, write the eye camera in order, so the last
   window that holds wins [verified, 0x00493a2c..0x00494aeb]:

    always        a default view from (u, 30, -110) toward (u + 50, -30, 0)
    -125 .. -5    an orbit of radius 7.5 about (u, -170, 0), up (0, 1, 1)
    -35 .. -25    the orbit at radius 52.5, angle 3.2 falling
    -25 .. -14    three orbits tightening and swinging
    -14 .. -5     the orbit easing out, then flying on with the bike
    -5 .. 0.5     a pull-back past the bike, easing by Math_Ease(0.4 t)
    0.5 .. 3.5    alongside, 60 out in z
    3.5 .. 6      rising behind it
    6 .. 9        from 80 out, sweeping back
    9 .. 14       climbing over the road
    14 .. 24.5    a long crane up and over, Math_Ease in four terms
    24.5 .. 35    from above, pulling back as 10 (13 - 1.2 (t - 25))

   The exact expressions are in the source and Frame's plate comment.

   5.3.  The shake and the light

   As part_video's (effects/video.txt 5.2), on the same try1.sav path:
   F, R = F x up, i = trunc(35 (t + 300000)) % 950, then target += R
   x_i b + up y_i b with b = 0.8 A, and up.x += sin(a_i * 1.7189 b)
   K * 0.3 M - this time not multiplied by 0. A is 0.011, 0.003 for
   -24 <= t < -17 and 0.005 after t = 6; K is 3 before t = -24, else
   0.5; M is 0.1, 0.01 or 0.07 by the same windows. bend3's RightV and
   UpV are x_i and y_i times K A 0.01 S, S = 0 until t = -20, -10.4
   for -17 < t < -14, else 3.99. a_i is Path_Load's smoothed column:
   with it the camera at t = 5.2476 reproduces 2.4's reading, up.x
   included [verified, 0x00494af1..0x00494f4e; observed]. The light
   camera is fixed: eye (0, 0, 30), target 0, up (0, 1, 0).

   [verified, every read of those fields in Init, Frame and DrawScene].
   No other part can use them: each part numbers its textures and
   programs in its own band - part_motor from 1000 (0x3e8), part_truck
   2000, part_rail 3000, part_boat 4000, part_end 5000, part_city
   below 0x100 - and loads its own copies, pattern5 included
   [verified, the slot assignments in each Init].

   It sets "alpha", "RightV" and "UpV" on the program in this+500
   (effects/look.txt 3.3), then draws this+0x1138 to the window with
   glBlendFunc(GL_ONE, GL_ONE) in a grey that fades in as
   clamp((t + 35) * 0.5) [verified]. alpha is

    x     = clamp((t + 30) * 0.4, 0, 1)
    alpha = (1 - Math_Ease(x, 1)) * 0.3 + 0.2

   and Math_Ease(x, 1) is (1 - cos(pi x)) / 2, so alpha eases from
   0.5 at the part's start to 0.2 by t = -27.5 [verified, Math_Ease's
   plate comment, and 0.2 observed at t = 5.2]. RightV and UpV are
   a per-frame table entry scaled by a factor that is 0 until t = -20
   [verified].

8.  Reconstruction notes

   The shader files are the demo's code and are transcribed as they
   are, dead assignments and all, with the live result noted. The rim
   term is per vertex and interpolated, not per pixel; keeping it per
   vertex keeps the faceted quality of the edges on coarse meshes.

   The port matches the original at every 6 s checkpoint from t = -30
   to 6 at the 800x450 view, to a mean of 0.12 per 8-bit channel or
   less [observed, the agent pipe on both].
