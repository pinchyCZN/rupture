RUPTURE

A Windows demo by Andromeda Software Development, and the winner of the PC demo competition at The Gathering 2009. It draws with OpenGL and Cg shaders to "Throw Navis Off The Train" by aMUSiC and Leviathan. pouet.net

This is not an emulator and not a capture. Every part is a transcription of the original's own code, in C++ on WebGL2, and every Cg shader the demo binds is transcribed to GLSL. It reads the release's own meshes, textures and music; where the demo hard-codes a number, so does this.

The differences are ours: the TV's 838 video frames are one MPEG-1 file, the music starts muted, and the look, zoom, fast forward and part skip are additions the demo does not have.

The source and the reverse engineering notes: github.com/pinchyCZN/rupture
